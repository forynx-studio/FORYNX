05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before we begin.

Do prices include domain and hosting?

No. Packages include website files and deployment assistance. Domain purchases, paid services and renewals are the client's responsibility.

Can I update products myself?

These are static websites. Updating products or copy requires editing and redeploying the files. An admin dashboard is quoted separately.

Do websites include online payments?

No. These packages cover informational sites and product/service showcases, without shopping carts or payment gateways.

How do we start, and how do payments work?

After discussing requirements, we sign a contract and collect a 50% deposit. The remaining 50% is due after approval and before final delivery.

How many revisions are included?

Each package includes 3 revisions within the agreed scope. Additional small revisions start at SAR 20; new features and redesigns are quoted separately.

Are Friday and Saturday counted as business days?

No. Friday and Saturday are excluded. The timeline starts after receiving all requirements and the deposit.

Do you guarantee top Google rankings?

No. We set up SEO fundamentals to help search engines understand your site, but cannot guarantee rankings or immediate indexing.
